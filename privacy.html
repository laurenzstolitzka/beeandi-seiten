Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Controller

Bee & I Productions e.U., Laurenz Stolitzka, Graf-Starhemberg-Gasse 2B/4, 1040 Vienna, Austria, email office.beeandi@gmail.com ("we"). This policy covers this website and our private software tool Mangole Clips Studio ("the tool"). We follow the GDPR and the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG).

2. This website

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open it, the host processes your IP address and technical request data in server logs to deliver the page and keep it secure (legal basis: our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We set no cookies and use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no external fonts or scripts.

3. Contact

The contact form on this site does not transmit data to us. It only opens your own email program with your text. If you email us, we process your name, email address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them when the matter is closed, unless law requires us to keep them (for example 7 years for accounting records under § 132 BAO).

4. The tool Mangole Clips Studio

The tool runs only on our own computer and is used only by us for our own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It makes short clips from livestreams of creators who permit it, writes captions and, after our manual review, uploads the clips to our own accounts. It stores access tokens and statistics of our own videos locally on our computer. It collects no data of other users of any platform, shares nothing with third parties, sells nothing, and uses no data for advertising.

YouTube / Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own channel, set thumbnails and subtitles, and read statistics of our own videos. By using it we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy applies. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok

When we connect our own TikTok account, the tool receives an access token. It uses it only to read the display name of that account and to upload our own videos to its drafts inbox (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). We publish in the TikTok app ourselves. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

Instagram and Facebook (Meta)

The tool uses the Instagram and Facebook Graph API with our own accounts to publish our own Reels and to read statistics of our own posts (for example views, reach, likes). No data of other users is read, except public profile and post data of public business accounts that Meta makes available through its API for research on which formats perform well; these are used only in aggregate and are not stored in personal form. Access can be revoked in the account settings of Instagram and Facebook under Apps and websites.

X (Twitter)

If we connect our own X account, the tool uses it only to prepare and publish our own posts. Access can be revoked in the settings of X under Security and account access, Apps and sessions.

Content Rewards and similar clipping platforms

To take part in clipping campaigns we submit links to our own published clips on the campaign platform, using our own account there.

5. Recipients and transfers

We do not sell personal data. The platforms named above and our website host act as independent controllers or processors under their own terms. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses where the provider uses them.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Contact us at office.beeandi@gmail.com. You may complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.

7. Deletion and revoking access

Locally stored tokens and statistics are deleted when you or we revoke the access on the platform and on request. We keep no copies in the cloud.

8. Changes

We update this policy when the tool or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
