Welcome to Bee & I Productions

A small film production company from Vienna. We specialise in drone video and in short-form clips of livestreams, made with the permission of the creators.

What we do

Drone video

Aerial footage for real estate, events, tourism and brands. Planned, licensed and flown in line with Austrian and EU drone rules.

Clipping for streamers

Short vertical clips of the best moments of livestreams, cut with subtitles for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. We only work with creators who allow it, for example through official clipping campaigns.

Film production

From idea to final cut: shooting, editing, colour and sound for companies and creators.

Our clipping tools

For our own channels we use a private software tool, Mangole Clips Studio. It runs on our own computer, prepares clips and, after a manual review, uploads them to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok (on TikTok only as a draft that we publish ourselves in the app). Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
