Mangole Clips Studio

A private tool of Bee & I Productions e.U. (Vienna) that prepares short gaming clips and saves them as drafts in the operator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to other users.

About

Mangole Clips Studio runs locally on the operator's own computer. It finds highlight moments in streams of creators who allow clipping (for example through official clipping campaigns such as Content Rewards), cuts vertical clips with subtitles and writes the captions. Every clip is reviewed and approved by the operator before anything leaves the computer.

Operated by Bee & I Productions e.U., Vienna, Austria. See the legal notice below.

How it works with TikTok

  • The operator connects the operator's own TikTok account once (TikTok Login Kit).
  • After the operator approves a clip locally, the tool uploads that one video file to the inbox of the operator's own account as a draft (Content Posting API, scope video.upload).
  • The operator opens the TikTok app, checks the draft and publishes it personally. The tool never publishes by itself.
  • The tool only reads the display name of the connected account (scope user.info.basic) to show which account is connected.
