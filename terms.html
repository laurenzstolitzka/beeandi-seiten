Last updated: 18 November 2026

Mangole Clips Studio is a private tool of Bee & I Productions e.U., Vienna, for preparing and uploading clips to its own accounts. It is not available to the public.

Use of TikTok

The tool uploads videos to TikTok only as drafts in the operator's own account. Publishing happens in the TikTok app by the operator, who complies with the TikTok Terms of Service, the Community Guidelines and the Developer Terms.

Content

Only clips from creators who have granted permission, for example through official clipping campaigns, are prepared. Every clip is reviewed and approved manually by the operator before it is uploaded. The tool enforces a daily limit and a minimum gap between uploads.
